Privacy Policy

Last updated August 26, 2026

The short version

Anify does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is nothing to opt out of, because nothing leaves your device.

Data we collect

None. Anify does not require an account, does not ask for your name or email, and does not read your contacts, location, camera, or photos.

Where your data lives

Anything you create inside the app — your library, watch/read progress, personal ratings, and notes — is stored locally on your device only. Uninstalling the app removes it permanently. We have no server that holds a copy.

Third-party services

Anify does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDK. No data is shared with, or sold to, anyone, because none is collected in the first place.

Children's privacy

Because Anify collects no personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect data from children either. The app is safe for use by any age group with respect to data collection.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example if a future version of Anify adds an optional online feature — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect it.
